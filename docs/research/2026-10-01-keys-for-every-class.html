Keys for every class and spec

Card 0082. Built 2026-10-01 11:06 from the addon's own tables (DjinnisClassProfiles 0.64.1) and Rob's key bindings. The script is docs/research/bars-analysis/keys_page.lua.

How to read this

  • Each table is one class. A row is one key. A column is one spec. The cell is the spell that goes on that key.
  • The jobs come from Bellular's planner. A job is a kind of spell, for example "Interrupt" or "Combat 1" (the main filler).
  • Your keys are the same in every spec, so "one job, one key" means each kind of spell is always on the same key.

White: Feral's key (the model)Blue: moved for the role (casters, healers, tanks)Green: a druid spec's own key, agreed with Rob Brown: a druid habit copied to another tank. Review it 

The rules used

  • Druid specs: the keys agreed so far (Feral is the model; Balance and Resto keep Sunfire 1, Moonfire 2, Wrath 3, Starfire 4; Guardian's own keys from 2026-10-01).
  • Melee damage (every other class): Feral's keys.
  • Ranged damage and healers: Feral's keys, but Combat 1 to 4 sit where Balance and Resto have them (3, 4, 2, 1), and Combat 7 on Alt+3.
  • Tanks: Guardian's keys, but not Prowl on Shift+3 (a druid's stealth). The taunt goes on Alt+2.
  • Healthstone and the damage potion are on the same key for every class.

The case against, before you read the tables

  • Casters' 1 to 4 are copied from Balance. Balance keeps Sunfire 1, Moonfire 2, Wrath 3, Starfire 4 because your hands know those. For a Fire Mage that means Scorch 1, Pyroblast 2, Fireball 3, Fire Blast 4. That order has no reason of its own. The other way: Combat 1 to 4 on keys 1 to 4 in order for every non-druid caster.
  • Tanks copy Guardian, and some Guardian keys are your druid habits (shown brown). The worst one: Combat 6 on T. For a Brewmaster that is Purifying Brew, and for a Protection Warrior Ignore Pain. Both are pressed often, and T is a stretch key.
  • Many jobs have no key yet. Your Feral bars never needed them (for example a dispel, a second crowd control, a raid defensive). Each spec lists them under "No key yet". They need keys before a healer or a caster can use this plan.
  • Bellular's "Combat" numbers mean different things in each class. So the rotation rows only line up roughly. The rows below the rotation (interrupt, movement, defensives, taunt) line up exactly, and they matter most.
