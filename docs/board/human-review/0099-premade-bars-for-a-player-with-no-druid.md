# 0099 Premade bars for a player with no druid

## Why

More > "Make premade bars from your druid's bars" (`PlanTab.barsFrom`) translates a druid's saved
bars into another spec's abilities by Bellular's job categories (cards `0051`, `0082`). A published
user with no druid has no template, so the feature gives them nothing.

## What I need from you

Pick 1 or 2.

## Options

1. **Hide the option when the account has no druid bars.** Cost: one check. A non-druid user loses
   the feature.
2. **Ship your druid layout inside the addon as the default template.** A player with no druid of
   their own gets premade bars built from yours. Cost: a generated block, and your key choices
   (card `0082`) become every user's starting point. Raises one more question: whether players can
   pick one of their own characters as the template instead.

## Recommendation

Option 2. It is the only one that gives a new user something on day one, and your layout has had
more thought than any default would.
